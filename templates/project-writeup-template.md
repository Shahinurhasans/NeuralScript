Copy this file, rename it, and replace each prompt in italics with your own writing. A good write-up lets a reader judge your work without running your code.

## Motivation

_What problem did you set out to solve, and why does it matter? One paragraph._

## Approach

_What did you build or try, and why this approach over the obvious alternatives? Add a diagram if the system has several parts._

## Results

_Report numbers in a table, with what a reader needs to interpret them: dataset, metric, baseline, and number of runs._

| Method     | Metric |  Notes                 |
| :--------- | -----: | :--------------------- |
| _Baseline_ | _0.00_ | _where it comes from_  |
| _Yours_    | _0.00_ | _what changed_         |

## What didn't work

_Ideas that failed, and what each one taught you. Readers trust results more when they can see the dead ends._

## What I learned

_The technical and research lessons you would carry into your next project._

## Code and data

_Link the repository, the dataset, and any trained models._
